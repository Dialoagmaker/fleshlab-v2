## VISUAL STRATEGY

### PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
- **Cinematic:** Moody lighting, shallow depth of field
- **Authentic:** Real performers, real environments
- **Aspirational:** Premium quality, not amateur
- **Diverse:** Asian market focus, but inclusive
- **Confident:** Direct eye contact, strong poses

### COLOR PALETTE
- **Primary:** Deep blacks, charcoals
- **Secondary:** Rich primary accent (red, electric blue, or purple)
- **Text:** High contrast white/light gray
- **Feel:** Netflix meets high-end adult studio

### TYPOGRAPHY
- **Headlines:** Bold, condensed sans-serif (impact)
- **Body:** Clean, readable sans-serif (clarity)
- **Hierarchy:** Massive headlines, minimal body copy
- **Rule:** If it can be an image, don't make it text

### LAYOUT PRINCIPLES
- **Full-bleed images:** Edge-to-edge visuals
- **Generous whitespace:** Let content breathe
- **Asymmetric layouts:** Dynamic, not grid-locked
- **Scroll-triggered animations:** Subtle motion
- **Mobile-first:** Design for phone first, desktop second
